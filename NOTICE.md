# Provenance

pi-accounts originated in [genzorr/agents](https://github.com/genzorr/agents); [genzorr/pi-accounts](https://github.com/genzorr/pi-accounts) is its canonical source. It is distributed under the [MIT license](LICENSE).

The tool uses OAuth, path and credential-storage primitives from the user's separately installed `@earendil-works/pi-coding-agent` and `@earendil-works/pi-ai`. Their source and licenses are not bundled here; see [Pi's upstream implementation and licensing](https://github.com/earendil-works/pi).

MIT-licensed codex-multi-auth 2.19.0 was consulted as a user-interface reference. No nontrivial source from it was incorporated.
